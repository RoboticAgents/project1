## Implementation Notes for Google Forms:

1. **Settings to configure:**
   - ✓ Limit to 1 response (using Allegheny email)
   - ✓ Collect email addresses
   - ✓ Do NOT share responses with respondents
   - ✓ Only you (instructor) can see responses
   - Set as "Required" for most questions (except those marked "Optional")

2. **Sharing the form:**
   - Share alongside peer evaluation form
   - Emphasize honesty and reflection
   - Due same time as peer evaluation (with final submission)

3. **Using responses:**
   - Compare self-assessment with peer evaluations
   - Look for alignment or discrepancies
   - Consider explanations for lower contributions
   - Use to inform code review questions
   - Holistic view of student's experience and learning

4. **Grading considerations:**
   - Self-reflection score (0.3 pts) based on:
     - Honesty and self-awareness
     - Depth of reflection on learning
     - Evidence of growth mindset
     - Not just on whether they contributed fully (that's assessed elsewhere)
